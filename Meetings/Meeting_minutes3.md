## Discussion

Jina presented paper sketches and low-fidelity prototypes for the login page, dashboard,
quiz layout, and difficulty selection screens. The team reviewed each design and provided
structured feedback.

Key suggestions raised:
- Improve navigation clarity between the dashboard and game pages
- Add brief level descriptions to the difficulty selector cards
- Timer bar should transition colour as time decreases (green → yellow → red)

The team agreed to add Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels across both Math and
Cyber Security quizzes to better serve the target age range (6–12 years).

Additional eco-themed cyber-security educational content was proposed and assigned to
Aaryut and Saif for development.

## Outcomes

- Initial wireframe designs approved with minor improvement notes
- Three difficulty tiers confirmed for all quiz categories
- All core sections confirmed: login, register, OTP verification, dashboard, quiz pages
- Timer bar colour transition added to development backlog
- Team agreed to proceed with the high-fidelity digital prototype
